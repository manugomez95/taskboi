# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
  and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct,
and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through
GitHub's **Report content** or user-blocking/reporting features when the conduct
occurred on GitHub. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. If the matter
is a repository security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

> **Maintainer decision required:** Manuel must designate a monitored, private
> conduct-reporting address or form and identify an alternate recipient for
> reports involving the primary maintainer. Until that route exists, the project
> cannot promise a project-controlled private intake channel; reporters may use
> GitHub's platform reporting tools without notifying the project.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters, disclose details
only as needed to investigate and act, avoid assigning a reported person to
handle their own case, and review incidents as promptly and fairly as practical.

Enforcement may range from a private correction or warning through temporary or
permanent exclusion, according to impact, intent, and repeated behavior.
