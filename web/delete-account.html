🗑️ Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your Taskboi account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

⚠️ Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What will be deleted:

  • Your user account and profile information
  • All projects you've created
  • All tasks and subtasks
  • All associated metadata and preferences

How to request deletion:

Send an email to the address below with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Taskboi account.

📧 Request Account Deletion

Email: support@taskboi.app

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.
